We have more thoughts than we have places to put them. Most of them never come back when we need them.

Magpie is where the half-formed stuff goes to become something. A collection of your curiosities, with a conversation partner who remembers.

How it works

Three dimensions. One simple shape.

Every thought finds its place in a wiki that grows with you. Subject. Topic. Facet. Add bullets when something hits, then explore from any angle.

01

Subject

The big buckets you care about. Books, history, AI, music, philosophy, whatever pulls you.

your curiosity, organized

02

Topic

A specific thread inside a subject. "Why every empire thinks it's the last one." Capture bullets as they come.

a conversation you'd want to have

03

Facet

Cross-cutting tags that connect topics across subjects. Paradoxes. Counterintuitive findings. Convergent ideas.

where unexpected connections live

Five modes

Five ways to look at any thought.

Every topic opens with the same five tabs. Capture, brief yourself, get challenged, find better questions, or just talk it through.

Maggie

Capture bullets as they come. Tap the mic and just talk. Maggie holds the thread until you're ready to do something with it.

Brief

A short, smart primer on the topic. The thing you wish a friend would send you before a dinner party.

Challenge

A hot take, a steelman, or a paradox you didn't see coming. Sharpens your thinking by pushing back on it.

Questions

Open questions you could ask anyone. The kind that make other people feel interesting, not interrogated.

Convo

Talk it through with Maggie. She remembers what you wrote, riffs back with her own takes, and never opens with "Great question!"

Linq iMessage front door

Capture anywhere. Even from iMessage.

The best thoughts come when you are not at your computer. Walking to the office. Mid-conversation at dinner. Half-asleep at 2 AM.

Text Magpie a thought, and Maggie drops it in the right topic. Native iMessage features (read receipts, voice notes, tapbacks, group chats) all work as expected. Falls back to RCS and SMS when iMessage is not available.

Built on Linq Blue, the iMessage and SMS layer trusted by 50,000+ teams. Magpie joins Poke on the Linq Blue roster.

Draw Out

Practice making someone else brilliant.

A role-play mode where you talk with a character who has something interesting to say but won't volunteer it. Your job is to draw it out.

At the end, you get scored on conversational generosity. Did the other person light up?

Be the most interesting person in the room by making everyone else feel like one.

Krava Privacy Infrastructure for AI

The privacy layer your wiki was missing.

A wiki of your real curiosity is more revealing than your search history. Magpie holds the half-formed stuff (your doubts, your contrarian takes, the late-night thoughts you have not figured out how to say out loud yet) and that data deserves serious infrastructure.

Every Maggie conversation flows through Krava. Passkey identity. AES-256-GCM encrypted memory. Inference inside NVIDIA H100 Trusted Execution Environments. Krava is architecturally incapable of seeing who you are, and your data is encrypted before it touches any database with a key only you hold.

Privacy as infrastructure. Not a pinky promise.
