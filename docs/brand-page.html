The idea

A personal conversation gym.

A magpie collects shiny things and chatters constantly. So do you. Build a grid of topics you care about, get a fresh angle in seconds, and talk them through until they are yours. The nest grows. The thoughts compound.

The mark

One bird, one shiny thing.

A long-tailed magpie, perched, mid-chatter, with a single iridescent dot just off her beak: the curiosity she just spotted. She works on dark, on a subtle lift, and inverted on light.

Typography

Editorial warmth, modern clarity.
